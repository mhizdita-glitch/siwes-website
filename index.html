Description about NICTM

he National Institute of Construction Technology and Management (NICTM) is a Federal Polytechnic-level institution located in Uromi, Edo State, Nigeria.
Established in 2014, NICTM is dedicated to training skilled manpower in construction,
environmental technology,
engineering,
applied science,
and related fields.
The institute offers specialized NBTE-accredited ND and HND programmes,
as well as intensive short-term certificates in trades like Carpentry, Plumbing, Concreting & Masonry, POP Works, and Tiling & Stone Pitching.
NICTM is fully accredited by the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE) and is committed to providing a practical,
workshop-driven campus life that prepares students for immediate employability upon graduation.
The institution also offers robust Part-Time and Evening schedules across all its ND and HND programmes for working professionals,
along with subsidized federal on-campus accommodations.

The National Institute of Construction Technology and Management (NICTM) is a Federal Polytechnic established in 2014 in response to the increasing collapse of buildings and infrastructure in Nigeria. It aims to train skilled entrepreneurs in construction and allied fields, focusing on technical vocational education and training (TVET). NICTM offers various programs, including the School of Engineering Technology, School of Environmental Technology, School of Applied Sciences, and School of Business Management Technology. The institution is fully accredited by the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE) and provides practical training to ensure graduates are employable upon graduation
