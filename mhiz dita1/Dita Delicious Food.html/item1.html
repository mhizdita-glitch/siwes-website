Yam and Egg Sauce

Yam and Egg Sauce is a delicious Nigerian meal made with fried yam served alongside a rich and spicy egg sauce. The egg sauce is prepared with fresh tomatoes, pepper, onions and eggs.

This meal is suitable for breakfast, lunch or dinner. It is freshly prepared and served as a satisfying meal.
