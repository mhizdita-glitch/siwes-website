Pounded Yam and Egusi Soup

Pounded Yam and Egusi Soup is a traditional Nigerian meal consisting of smooth pounded yam served with rich egusi soup. The soup is prepared with ground melon seeds, vegetables, spices and assorted protein.

It is a delicious and filling meal that can be enjoyed for lunch or dinner.
