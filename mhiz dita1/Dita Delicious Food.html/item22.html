Yam and Fish Stew

Yam and Fish Stew is a delicious Nigerian meal consisting of soft swallow served with rich fish stew. The stew is prepared with fresh tomatoes, peppers, onions and tasty spices.

The meal is freshly prepared and provides a satisfying traditional food experience. It is suitable for lunch, dinner and family meals.
