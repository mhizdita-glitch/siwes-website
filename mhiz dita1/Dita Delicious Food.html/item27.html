Vegetable Soup and Eba

Vegetable Soup and Eba is a traditional Nigerian meal prepared with fresh leafy vegetables, peppers, seasonings and delicious protein, served with smooth pounded yam.

The soup is rich and flavourful, while the soft Eba makes the meal filling and satisfying. It is freshly prepared and is suitable for lunch or dinner.
